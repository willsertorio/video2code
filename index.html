Overview

One video clip, rebuilt as code two ways: by Gemini 4 Carbon from a single prompt, and by hand. The sample page plays the original and both code versions side by side, locked to the same frame.

Prompt sent to Gemini 4 Carbon

transform this video into code, be the more realistic as you can

  1. 1

    Who
    Source clip

    Output
    • 591 frames
    • The reference the two code versions are measured against
  2. 2

    Gemini 4 Carbon

    Who
    google/gemini-4-carbon, through OpenRouter
    What
    Received a 720 x 1280 copy of the clip with the prompt "transform this video into code, be the more realistic as you can", in one call with no follow-up
    Output
    • carbon.html, 39 KB
    • A ray-marched sky and a simulated flock of 19 birds, drawn from scratch
  3. 3

    Human

    Who
    Human
    What
    Measured the clip, then rebuilt it from those measurements: a stitched sky map, the camera path, the lens shading and every bird traced in every frame
    Output
    • human.html, 4.9 MB
    • 43.98 dB PSNR and 0.992 SSIM against the source, over all 591 frames
