Overview

One video clip, rebuilt as code two ways: by Gemini 4 Carbon from a single prompt, and by hand. The sample page plays the original and both code versions side by side, locked to the same frame. A second clip, Valley Flyover, was put through the same prompt and is covered at the end.

Prompt sent to Gemini 4 Carbon

transform this video into code, be the more realistic as you can

  1. 1

    Original video

    Who
    Source clip
    What
    A 19.7-second handheld clip of geese crossing a sunset sky, filmed at 2160 x 3840 and 29.97 fps
    Output
    • 591 frames
    • The reference the two code versions are measured against
  2. 2

    Gemini 4 Carbon

    Who
    google/gemini-4-carbon, through OpenRouter
    What
    Received a 720 x 1280 copy of the clip with the prompt "transform this video into code, be the more realistic as you can", in one call with no follow-up
    Output
    • carbon.html, 39 KB
    • A ray-marched sky and a simulated flock of 19 birds, drawn from scratch
  3. 3

    Human

    Who
    Human
    What
    Measured the clip, then rebuilt it from those measurements: a stitched sky map, the camera path, the lens shading and every bird traced in every frame
    Output
    • human.html, 4.9 MB
    • 43.98 dB PSNR and 0.992 SSIM against the source, over all 591 frames

Valley Flyover

A second clip: a 19.3-second drone flight over forested hills (1920 x 1080, 24 fps). Gemini 4 Carbon got the same prompt and answered with a landscape generated by a shader. The Human version is a 3D scene rebuilt from the footage: 78 keyframes with recovered depth and the recovered flight path. Its sample page plays the original and both code versions side by side and shows the rubric written for the clip, scored on both by Gemini 4 Carbon.
